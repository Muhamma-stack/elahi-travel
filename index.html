Frequently Asked Questions

Booking & Travel Questions

? Where does the bus depart from and at what time?
All Riyadh packages start from Batha (Old Saptco Bus Station). Daily departures are available — the exact timing is confirmed on WhatsApp once your travel date is booked.
? Why are prices not shown on the website?
The rate depends on the season, your dates, the bus type (VIP or Normal), the hotel category and the total number of passengers. That is why we confirm the exact best rate with you through the Book Now form or on WhatsApp — with no hidden charges.
? What is the difference between the VIP Bus and the Normal Bus?
Both buses are air-conditioned and clean. The VIP Bus offers more legroom, better seating and a premium interior, making it more comfortable for longer journeys. The Normal Bus is the budget-friendly option.
? Can you also book a hotel for us?
Yes. You can choose a 3 Star, 4 Star or 5 Star hotel — options close to the Haram are available in both Makkah and Madinah. If you only need the bus, there is also a “No Hotel (bus only)” option.
? Is there a discount for families?
Yes — a special family discount is available. The larger your group, the better the rate can be. Enter the number of men and women in the booking form and we will send you our best family rate.
? What is included in a Pakistan package?
Pakistan packages can include flight + visa + hotel + transport. Options range from 7 to 40 days. Exact inclusions are confirmed through the booking form or on WhatsApp so there is no confusion.
? How do I make a booking?
Go to the Packages page, choose your package and click Book Now. Once you submit the form your details are sent to us on WhatsApp. Or simply message us directly on +966 53 632 1755.
